Plaintext,

A fullscreen-first editor for clear writing and the pleasure of working on one thing at a time.

Download beta for macOS View source

v0.1.0-beta.1 · macOS 14 or later

One document

No tabs, no file library.

Only the words

No formatting, syntax highlighting, or spellcheck.

Quiet by default

Fullscreen opens first. The canvas has a fixed reading width, generous line space, and a choice of six typefaces.

Local and recoverable

Autosave and local version history protect the work. Plaintext has no accounts, sync, or network use.
