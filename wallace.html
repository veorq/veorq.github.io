Selected essays and nonfiction, with direct links to the texts. Some are hosted by their publishers; others are transcriptions or scans. Links checked on October 14, 2026.

Essays in PDF

  • Big Red Son (PDF scan)
    The adult film industry's awards in Las Vegas. Originally published in Esquire as “Neither Adult Nor Entertainment”; scan hosted by the publisher.
  • Consider the Lobster (PDF)
    Maine's lobster festival and the ethics of eating animals. Gourmet, 2004; hosted by East Tennessee State University.
  • Deciderization 2007—a Special Report (PDF)
    Choosing essays and making sense of information. Introduction to The Best American Essays 2007; hosted by neugierig.org.
  • E Unibus Pluram: Television and U.S. Fiction (PDF scan)
    Television, irony, and American fiction. Review of Contemporary Fiction, 1993; hosted on James Somers's personal website.
  • Fictional Futures and the Conspicuously Young (PDF)
    Young American writers and the influence of television. Review of Contemporary Fiction, 1988; hosted by neugierig.org.
  • The Weasel, Twelve Monkeys and the Shrub (PDF)
    On the road with John McCain's 2000 presidential campaign. Rolling Stone article; WordPress-hosted copy.
  • The View from Mrs. Thompson's (PDF scan)
    September 11 as experienced in Bloomington, Illinois. Also published as “9/11: The View from the Midwest.” WordPress-hosted scan; pages are sideways.

Essays on the web

  • Tense Present: Democracy, English, and the Wars over Usage
    English usage, authority, and Bryan Garner's dictionary. Harper's, April 2001 version, later collected as “Authority and American Usage”; hosted by California State University, Bakersfield.
  • David Lynch Keeps His Head
    On the set of Lost Highway, and on Lynch's films. Reproduction of the Premiere article, 1996, at Library of Babel.
  • The Nature of the Fun
    Writing fiction, ambition, and the changing pleasures of the work. Transcription at Michael Fuchs's Dispatch from the Razor's Edge.
  • F/X Porn
    Special effects and the films of James Cameron. Transcription at Michael Fuchs's Dispatch from the Razor's Edge.
