Essays in PDF

    The adult film industry's awards in Las Vegas. Originally published in Esquire as “Neither Adult Nor Entertainment”.

    Maine's lobster festival and the ethics of eating animals. Gourmet, 2004.
  • Deciderization 2007—a Special Report (PDF)
    Choosing essays and making sense of information. Introduction to The Best American Essays 2007.
  • E Unibus Pluram: Television and U.S. Fiction (PDF scan)
    Television, irony, and American fiction. Review of Contemporary Fiction, 1993.
  • Fictional Futures and the Conspicuously Young (PDF)
    Young American writers and the influence of television. Review of Contemporary Fiction, 1988.
  • The Weasel, Twelve Monkeys and the Shrub (PDF)

  • The View from Mrs. Thompson's (PDF scan)
    September 11 as experienced in Bloomington, Illinois. Also published as “9/11: The View from the Midwest.”
